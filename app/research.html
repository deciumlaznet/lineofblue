Research and design

The evidence behind Line of Blue

Line of Blue supports the whole job of national police communications: speaking for policing on government policy, keeping public confidence, working with the media and partners, and putting the verified facts out first when something goes wrong. Every screen, workflow and safeguard traces back to published law, national guidance, inspection findings, and what police and governments around the world have learned the hard way.

The whole job

Managing damage is one part of a much wider remit

A national police communications team speaks for policing on government policy, builds confidence through campaigns, runs a national press office, keeps 43 force teams aligned, and works with partners from the Home Office to the platforms. Crisis work is where minutes matter most, so the demo starts there, but the product is designed for all of it.

Policy and government

Speaking for policing on policy means treading a fine line

Chiefs answer to the law, not to ministers, for operational decisions. On policy, policing sometimes co-owns the plan, sometimes backs it with conditions, sometimes challenges it, and always protects its impartiality. The examples below are real positions taken in public.

    Incidents worldwide

    What good and bad handling look like, on every continent

    Twenty-seven incidents from Europe, the Americas, Asia, Africa and Oceania, each documented by an inquiry, court, review or reputable reporting. Each is judged on how the authorities communicated, not on the incident itself, and each leaves a lesson that shaped Line of Blue.

    How it was designed

    Requirements engineered from the people who will use it

    Line of Blue was specified by working outward from the people involved in a live incident: what each of them needs to know, decide and sign off, and how fast. Each stage fed the next.

    Who does the work

    Agents take the volume work. People keep every decision that is public, legal or about people. Whatever the level, the agent never publishes, contacts a third party or profiles a person on its own.
